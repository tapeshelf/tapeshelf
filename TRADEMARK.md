# Trademark policy

The code of TapeShelf is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. The license
covers the code; it does not grant rights to the name **tapeshelf** or to the tapeshelf logo (the play
button standing on a shelf with a leaning tape). This page explains what you may do with them.

## You may, without asking

- Run your own instance of unmodified or modified TapeShelf and say that it runs TapeShelf.
- Keep the name and logo that ship with the software in your instance's interface, e-mails and contact
  sheets, or replace them with your own (`SITE_NAME`).
- Write about TapeShelf, link to it, review it, teach it, package it for a distribution or an app catalog,
  and use the name to describe compatibility ("works with TapeShelf").

## Please don't

- Present your instance as the official service at tapeshelf.com, or suggest that it is operated,
  endorsed or supported by the TapeShelf project.
- Sell hosting, support or a product under the TapeShelf name or logo, or use them in your own product
  or company name, domain name or app-store listing.
- Publish a modified fork under the TapeShelf name. Forks are welcome; give yours a different name and
  logo, and you are free to say that it is based on TapeShelf.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether your use is fine, open a discussion in the repository before you go ahead.
